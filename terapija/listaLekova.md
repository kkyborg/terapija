# RULES

- Eutirox: 1x per day; 4 days = 50mg + 3 days = 75mg; must be taken 30-60 minutes before taking the food and any other medicines; preferrably early in the morning
- Aleract: 2x per day
- Inofolic Combi: 2x per day
- Pronison 5mg: 1x per day (recommended after breakfast)
- Utrogestan 200mg: 3x per day; must be taken at least 1-2h apart from Nifelat
- Nifelat: 3x per day; must be taken at least 1-2h apart from Utrogestan
- Prenatal Esential: 1x per day
- Heferal: 2x per day; on empty stomach: 1h before breakfast and 2h after dinner; NOT with milk NOR calcium; with C vitamin
- Vitamin C: 2x; take together with Heferal
- Glukophage 1000xr: 1x per day; with dinner
